## Overview
- **Full Name:** Kael Lightinger
- **Aliases:** The Orphan, The Red-Haired, Ginger, Lightinger
- **Age:** 15
- **Role:** Protagonist / The Last Vessel
- **Status:** Infected (The Feverlight - Stage 1)

---

## Appearance
- **Hair:** Messy, vibrant red. It’s his most defining feature and the source of his "Lightinger" nickname. In moments of high stress, the strands seem to hum or stand on end.
- **Build:** Lanky and awkward, still growing into his frame. He looks "stretched thin", both physically and mentally.
- **Eyes:** Wide and alert, but rimmed with the redness of the Fever. He has the "thousand-yard stare" of someone twice his age.
- **Distinction:** He often keeps his hands shoved in his pockets or wrapped in rags, terrified of what might happen if he accidentally touches someone and triggers a "Memory Leak."

---

## Personality & Motivation
- **The Reluctant Prodigy:** Kael doesn't want the **Elder Magic**. He views it as a curse that killed his family and stole his future.
- **Core Motivation:** Survival and Protection. He is fiercely protective of his little sister—she is the only person who doesn't look at him like he's a ticking time bomb.
- **The Burnout:** Even at 16, he is emotionally exhausted. He’s tired of running, tired of being feared, and tired of the constant "noise" of the memories he absorbs.
- **Temperament:** Sarcastic and defensive. He uses wit as a shield to keep people at a distance so they don't get hurt by his infection.

---

## Magic & Abilities
### **The Feverlight (The Catalyst)**
The disease acts as a bridge between Kael’s physical body and the "Source." While others get simple mutations, Kael’s infection opened a door to the **Elder Magic**.

### **Elder Magic: Psychometric Resonance**
- **The Power:** Kael perceives the "Truth" of objects and people through touch. 
- **Memory Echoes:** When he touched his buddy's shoulder, he didn't just "see" a memory; he *lived* it. He felt the cold, the smells, and the emotions of a moment that wasn't his.
- **The Danger:** He cannot always control the intake. Touching an old ruin might flood his brain with centuries of history, causing a physical seizure or a "Fever spike."
- **The Mutation:** His red hair is slowly becoming "conductive." It’s no longer just hair; it’s a sensory organ for the Elder Magic.

---

## Background
- **The Tragedy:** His family died during a Feverlight outbreak. As the sole survivor, the village branded him "The Orphan"—a reminder that he belongs to no one and nowhere.
- **The Incident:** During a confrontation where his sister was threatened, Kael’s "Fever" broke. He touched an assailant and released a surge of Elder Magic that didn't just push the man back—it "echoed" the man's own fear back into him so hard it paralyzed him.
- **The Flight:** Now a fugitive, he is hunted by the **White Order** because a 16-year-old with the power to rewrite reality is their greatest nightmare.
