**Working title:** *The Feverlight*

In a world called **Eryndor**, magic is known as **the Feverlight.**

It spreads from person to person like a sickness. At first, it gives people impossible abilities. Some can heal wounds. Some can speak to shadows. Some can control fire, glass, memory, or storms.

But every use of magic changes the infected person.

A healer may slowly become unable to feel pain.
A firecaster’s blood may start glowing like embers.
A shadow-speaker may begin forgetting their own face.

The world fears magic because nobody fully controls it.

# Main character

**Name:** Kael Veyr
**Age:** 15 or 16
**Role:** a normal village boy who works as a messenger
**Personality:** clever, loyal, stubborn, scared of becoming useless
**Big flaw:** he hides problems until they become dangerous
**Secret:** he has caught a rare type of Feverlight thought to be extinct.

Kael’s magic is called **Echo Magic.**

He can hear the memories left behind in places, objects, and people. At first it seems harmless. Then he starts hearing memories that have not happened yet.

# The main problem

People infected with Feverlight are usually taken away by an organization called **The White Order**.

The White Order says they protect society by treating infected people.

But many who are taken never return.

When Kael’s little sister gets sick with ordinary fever, Kael accidentally uses magic to save her. The village sees it. The White Order is called.

Kael runs.

Now he must find out what his magic really is before it destroys him — or before the White Order turns him into a weapon.

# The magical disease

The Feverlight has three stages:

## Stage 1: Spark

Small symptoms. Strange dreams. Glowing veins. Hearing whispers. Sudden abilities.

## Stage 2: Bloom

The magic becomes stronger. The person gains real power, but physical changes begin.

## Stage 3: Hollowing

The magic starts replacing part of the person’s humanity. They may lose memories, emotions, their body, or their identity.

People at Stage 3 are called **Hollows**.

Some become monsters. Some become almost angel-like. Some are still human inside, trapped in a changed body.

# Main characters around Kael
## Mira Solen
A girl who escaped the White Order.

Her magic lets her bend metal, but every time she uses it, her bones become more fragile. She acts tough because she is terrified of breaking.

## Rowan Vale
A young healer who secretly works against the White Order.

He believes Feverlight is not evil — it is misunderstood. But he may know more than he tells Kael.

## Sister Elian
A high-ranking member of the White Order.

She is calm, kind, and terrifying. She truly believes taking infected people away is mercy.

## The Hollow Saint
A legendary infected person who reached Stage 3 but did not become a monster.

Some say the Hollow Saint found a cure. Others say they became the source of the disease.

# Core theme

The story is about **fear of becoming something people don’t understand**.

Kael does not just fear dying.
He fears changing.
He fears people looking at him like he is a monster.
He fears that using his gift to help others might slowly destroy him.

# Possible opening scene

Kael is delivering medicine during a storm. His little sister is sick at home. The village doctor says she may not survive the night.

Kael grabs her hand and begs her to stay.

Then he hears something impossible: her heartbeat from yesterday, stronger and healthier.

Without knowing how, he pulls that memory into the present.

Her fever breaks.

But Kael’s veins flash silver.

His mother sees.

The village priest sees.

And by morning, the White Order’s bells are ringing.
