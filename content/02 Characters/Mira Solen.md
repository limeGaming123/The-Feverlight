# Mira Solen

**Role:** ally / foil  
**Magic:** metal-bending  
**Cost:** her bones become more fragile each time she uses her gift

Mira escaped [[03 World/The White Order|The White Order]]. She acts tough because she is terrified of breaking.

## Surface personality

Sharp, brave, sarcastic, impatient.

## Inner fear

She believes everyone eventually sees infected people as tools, weapons, or problems.

## Relationship to Kael

Mira thinks Kael is dangerously naive. Kael thinks Mira is too hard on everyone.

They are both right.

## Possible arc

Mira starts as someone who survives alone. She ends by choosing to protect someone even when it costs her.
