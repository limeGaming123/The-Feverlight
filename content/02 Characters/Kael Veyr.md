# Kael Veyr

**Role:** protagonist  
**Age:** 15 or 16  
**Home:** [[03 World/Brindlemark]]  
**Magic:** [[01 Story Bible/Echo Magic]]

Kael is a village messenger who is clever, loyal, stubborn, and scared of becoming useless. His biggest flaw is that he hides problems until they become dangerous.

## Want

Kael wants his family to be safe and normal again.

## Need

Kael needs to accept that hiding his sickness only gives other people more control over him.

## Secret

Kael has caught a rare type of Feverlight thought to be extinct.

## Opening wound

When his sister [[02 Characters/Lira Veyr|Lira]] is dying, Kael saves her by pulling the memory of her healthy heartbeat into the present.

This is the moment his life breaks open.

## Relationships

- [[02 Characters/Lira Veyr]] — his little sister and emotional anchor.
- [[02 Characters/Mira Solen]] — escaped infected girl who teaches him survival.
- [[02 Characters/Rowan Vale]] — healer who knows more than he admits.
- [[02 Characters/Sister Elian]] — antagonist who believes capturing him is mercy.

## Character arc

Kael begins by seeing his magic as a curse that must be hidden.

By the end of Book One, he understands that the real danger is not having power. The danger is letting fear decide who gets to use it.
