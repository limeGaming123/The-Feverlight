# Rowan Vale

**Role:** healer / mentor / possible betrayer  
**Public identity:** wandering physician  
**Secret:** former White Order researcher

Rowan believes the Feverlight is not evil. He also knows that belief does not make it safe.

## Strength

He understands symptoms, stages, and old cases better than almost anyone outside the Order.

## Weakness

He hides information because he thinks people cannot handle the full truth.

## Connection

Rowan may have studied old records about [[03 World/The Hollow Saint|The Hollow Saint]].
