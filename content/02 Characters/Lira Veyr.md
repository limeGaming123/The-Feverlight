# Lira Veyr

**Role:** Kael's little sister  
**Importance:** emotional trigger for the plot

Lira is the person [[02 Characters/Kael Veyr|Kael]] saves in the opening chapter.

## Story function

She proves that Kael's magic can do good.

She also proves that doing good can expose him.

## Possible future twist

When Kael saves her, he may leave a tiny echo of his magic inside her.
