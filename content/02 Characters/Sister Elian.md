# Sister Elian

**Role:** main antagonist  
**Faction:** [[03 World/The White Order]]  
**Belief:** containment is mercy

Sister Elian is calm, kind, and terrifying.

She does not see herself as cruel. She believes taking infected people away prevents families, villages, and kingdoms from being destroyed.

## Why she works

The best version of Elian is not evil for no reason. She has seen what uncontrolled Hollowing can do.

## Conflict with Kael

Kael represents everything she fears: a rare infection, uncontrolled magic, and a power the Order cannot predict.

## Possible private grief

Someone she loved may have become a Hollow.
