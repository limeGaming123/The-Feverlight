# The Three Stages

The Feverlight has three known stages.

## Stage 1: Spark

Small symptoms. Strange dreams. Glowing veins. Whispering sounds. Sudden abilities.

Most infected people are discovered here.

Symptoms:

- fever without illness
- silver, gold, red, or violet light under the skin
- dreams that feel like warnings
- magic appearing during fear or stress

## Stage 2: Bloom

The magic becomes stronger. The person gains real power, but physical and emotional changes begin.

Examples:

- a healer becomes numb to pain
- a firecaster cannot cry normal tears
- an echo-listener hears voices in empty rooms

## Stage 3: Hollowing

The magic starts replacing part of the person's humanity. They may lose memories, emotions, their body, or their identity.

People at Stage 3 are called **Hollows**.

Some become monsters. Some become almost angel-like. Some are still human inside, trapped in a changed body.

## The official view

[[03 World/The White Order|The White Order]] teaches that Hollowing is irreversible.

## The forbidden view

Followers of [[03 World/The Hollow Saint|The Hollow Saint]] believe Hollowing is not the end.

They believe it is a doorway.
