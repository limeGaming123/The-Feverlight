# Magic System — The Feverlight

The Feverlight is both a disease and a source of magic.

It is feared because it gives power before it takes payment.

## What it does

When someone catches the Feverlight, it awakens a magical ability based on something already deep inside them: fear, grief, obsession, desire, memory, guilt, faith, hunger, or love.

The magic is personal.

A grieving mother may speak with echoes of the dead.  
A soldier afraid of pain may grow skin like iron.  
A liar may become able to bend voices.  
A child obsessed with birds may grow glass-feathered wings.

## The cost

Every use pushes the infected person deeper into [[01 Story Bible/The Three Stages|the three stages]]. The cost should match the gift.

| Gift | Possible cost |
|---|---|
| Healing | Losing the ability to feel pain |
| Fire | Blood and tears heat into embers |
| Shadows | Forgetting your own reflection |
| Echo Magic | Losing the difference between memory and reality |

## How it spreads

The exact cause is debated.

Common theories:

- blood contact with an infected person
- close exposure during a magical outburst
- old relics connected to [[03 World/The Hollow Saint|The Hollow Saint]]
- deliberate infection by [[03 World/The White Order|The White Order]] for experiments

## What makes Kael different

[[02 Characters/Kael Veyr|Kael]] does not simply remember the past. He can pull an earlier memory of something into the present.

That means his gift may not be memory magic.

It may be time magic wearing a safer name.

> [!warning]
> Rule for the story: magic should solve one problem while creating a bigger one.
