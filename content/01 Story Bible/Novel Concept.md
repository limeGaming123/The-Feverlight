# Novel Concept

**Working title:** *The Feverlight*

In the world of [[03 World/Eryndor|Eryndor]], magic is known as **the Feverlight**.

It spreads from person to person like a sickness. At first, it gives people impossible abilities. Some can heal wounds. Some can speak to shadows. Some can control fire, glass, memory, or storms.

But every use of magic changes the infected person.

A healer may slowly become unable to feel pain.  
A firecaster's blood may start glowing like embers.  
A shadow-speaker may begin forgetting their own face.

The world fears magic because nobody fully controls it.

## Main promise

This is a dark fantasy story where magic feels beautiful, useful, and terrifying at the same time.

The reader should constantly ask:

> Is the power worth what it costs?

## Main character

[[02 Characters/Kael Veyr|Kael Veyr]] is a village messenger who catches a rare type of Feverlight called [[01 Story Bible/Echo Magic|Echo Magic]]. He can hear memories left inside places, objects, and people.

At first, it seems harmless.

Then he starts hearing memories that have not happened yet.

## Central conflict

People infected with Feverlight are usually taken away by [[03 World/The White Order|The White Order]]. The Order says they protect society by treating the infected.

But many who are taken never return.

When Kael accidentally uses magic to save his little sister [[02 Characters/Lira Veyr|Lira]], the village sees it. The White Order is called.

Kael runs.

## Core theme

The story is about fear of becoming something people do not understand.

Kael does not just fear dying. He fears changing. He fears people looking at him like he is a monster. He fears that using his gift to help others might slowly destroy him.
