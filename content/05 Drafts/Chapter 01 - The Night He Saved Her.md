# Chapter 01 — The Night He Saved Her

Kael Veyr first caught the Feverlight on the night he saved his sister's life.

He did not know it then. He only knew that [[02 Characters/Lira Veyr|Lira]]'s hand was too hot in his, that her breathing had become thin and uneven, and that his mother was crying in the corner where she thought he could not see.

Outside, rain hammered the roof of their cottage hard enough to drown the village bells. Inside, death sat at the foot of his sister's bed and waited.

Kael hated how quiet the room had become.

Lira was never quiet. She sang while sweeping ashes from the hearth. She asked questions while chewing bread. She laughed at her own jokes before anyone else had time to understand them.

Now she only breathed.

Once.

Then not for too long.

Then once again.

"Stay," Kael whispered.

His mother looked up. "Kael."

He ignored the warning in her voice and held Lira's hand tighter. Her fingers felt small and wrong, like a bird fallen from a nest.

"Stay," he said again.

Something answered.

It was not a voice. Not exactly.

It was a sound beneath sound: a memory trapped in the room. Lira laughing three mornings ago. Lira coughing yesterday. Lira's heartbeat from before the fever, strong and stubborn and alive.

Kael heard it as clearly as the rain.

Thump. Thump. Thump.

He reached for it without knowing what reaching meant.

The cottage candle flared silver.

Lira gasped.

Her back arched from the bed. The fever heat vanished from her skin so quickly that Kael almost dropped her hand.

His mother screamed.

Kael looked down.

Thin lines of silver light were crawling under his skin.

And from the chapel hill, through the storm, the village bell began to ring.

Not once.

Not for prayer.

Three times.

For infection.
