# Glossary

## Feverlight

The magical disease that grants power and slowly changes the infected.

## Spark

The first stage of infection.

## Bloom

The second stage, when power strengthens and visible changes begin.

## Hollowing

The third stage, when magic starts replacing humanity.

## Hollow

A person transformed by advanced Feverlight.

## Echo Magic

Kael's rare ability to hear and manipulate memories.

## The White Order

The organization that controls infected people in the name of public safety.
