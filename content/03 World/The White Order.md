# The White Order

The White Order is the organization responsible for identifying, removing, studying, and supposedly treating people infected with [[01 Story Bible/Magic System - The Feverlight|the Feverlight]].

## Public image

They wear white, speak softly, and arrive with bells.

They teach that they are healers.

## Private reality

Some infected people are helped. Some are imprisoned. Some disappear into research houses.

## Why people obey them

The Order has saved villages from Hollows before. That history gives them power.

## Key member

- [[02 Characters/Sister Elian]]

## Story purpose

The Order should feel believable. They are not wrong that Feverlight is dangerous. They are wrong to treat infected people as property.
