# The Hollow Saint

The Hollow Saint is a legendary infected person who reached [[01 Story Bible/The Three Stages#Stage 3 Hollowing|Stage 3]] but did not become a monster.

Some say the Saint found a cure.

Others say the Saint became the source of the Feverlight.

## Competing legends

1. The Saint healed a plague city and took the sickness into their own body.
2. The Saint tried to become immortal and cracked the world open.
3. The Saint is still alive inside every infected person.

## Connection to Kael

Kael's [[01 Story Bible/Echo Magic|Echo Magic]] may be the same magic the Saint had.

That makes him valuable to [[03 World/The White Order|The White Order]].
