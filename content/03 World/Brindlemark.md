# Brindlemark

Brindlemark is Kael's home village.

It is small enough that everyone knows when someone is sick, and frightened enough that everyone knows what to do when the White Order bells ring.

## Important places

- Kael's cottage
- the village bell tower
- the apothecary road
- the chapel house
- the old bridge where infected travelers used to be stopped

## Opening use

The first chapter begins here during a storm, when [[02 Characters/Lira Veyr|Lira]] is dying and [[02 Characters/Kael Veyr|Kael]] first uses [[01 Story Bible/Echo Magic]].
