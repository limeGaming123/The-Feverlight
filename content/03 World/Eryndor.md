# Eryndor

Eryndor is a world shaped by fear of magic.

Villages are built around bell towers. Cities have quarantine gates. Roads carry both merchants and White Order patrols.

## Social rule

People are polite about sickness until it becomes magical.

Then politeness ends.

## Important locations

- [[03 World/Brindlemark]] — Kael's village.
- [[03 World/The White Order]] — not a place only, but a religious-medical power structure.
- [[03 World/The Hollow Saint]] — the legend at the center of the oldest Feverlight stories.

## Mood

Rainy roads, candlelit houses, white stone sanctuaries, rusted quarantine bells, silver veins under skin.
