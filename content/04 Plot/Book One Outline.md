# Book One Outline

## Act I — The infection

1. [[05 Drafts/Chapter 01 - The Night He Saved Her|The Night He Saved Her]] — Kael saves Lira and exposes his magic.
2. The bells ring in [[03 World/Brindlemark]].
3. [[03 World/The White Order]] arrives with [[02 Characters/Sister Elian]].
4. Kael runs before he can be taken.

## Act II — The road

1. Kael meets [[02 Characters/Mira Solen]], who has already escaped the Order.
2. Kael learns about [[01 Story Bible/The Three Stages|Spark, Bloom, and Hollowing]].
3. [[02 Characters/Rowan Vale]] offers help, but hides his past.
4. Kael hears a future memory of himself begging him not to trust Rowan.

## Act III — The truth

1. The group finds forbidden records about [[03 World/The Hollow Saint]].
2. Kael realizes Echo Magic can change more than memories.
3. Sister Elian catches up and offers a choice: surrender or watch others suffer.
4. Kael uses his power publicly, saving people but pushing himself toward Bloom.

## Ending image

Kael hears a memory from the future.

It is his own voice saying:

> You already did this once. Do not let her die again.
