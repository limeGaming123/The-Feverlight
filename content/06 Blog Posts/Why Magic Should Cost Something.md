# Why Magic Should Cost Something

I like fantasy magic most when it is not just a superpower.

In **The Feverlight**, magic can save someone. It can heal, protect, reveal, and transform. But it never arrives for free.

That cost makes every magical moment a character choice.

When [[02 Characters/Kael Veyr|Kael]] uses [[01 Story Bible/Echo Magic|Echo Magic]], the question is not only:

> Can he do it?

The better question is:

> What will it take from him if he does?

That is what makes the story interesting to me. A power that only helps is useful. A power that helps and harms is dramatic.
